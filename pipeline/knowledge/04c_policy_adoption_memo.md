*Internal Memo*

**TO:      **All Propellant Media Employees

**FROM:  **Justin Croxton, CEO

**DATE:   **April 24, 2026

**RE:       ****Sexual Harassment Prevention Policy — Adoption and Training Requirements**

Team,

Today we are formally adopting the Propellant Media Sexual Harassment Prevention Policy, effective April 24, 2026. This policy has been attached to this memo and is also available in our shared drive. Please read it carefully.

This is both a legal requirement and a reflection of the kind of workplace I want us to be. A respectful, harassment-free environment is non-negotiable. Every person here — regardless of role, tenure, or title — has the right to work in a space free from sexual harassment, and every person here has the responsibility to help maintain it.

**What This Means for You**

- **Read the policy. **All 25 of you will receive a copy of the full policy attached to this memo. Take 15 minutes to read it end to end.

- **Sign the acknowledgment form. **Appendix B at the back of the policy is an acknowledgment form. Sign it, date it, and return it to me within 10 business days (by May 8, 2026). I will keep a copy in your personnel file.

- **Complete annual training. **We will be using the New York State Department of Labor's free online sexual harassment prevention training, which is fully compliant with New York Labor Law § 201-g. You will receive a separate email this week with the training link and a completion deadline. Training takes approximately 45 minutes and must be completed annually.

- **Know your reporting channels. **If you experience or witness behavior that may constitute sexual harassment, you can report it to me directly, or to Brent LeVasseur as the Backup Contact. Both of our contact details are in Section 7 of the policy. Reports can be made verbally or in writing. A complaint form is included as Appendix A if you prefer a written template, but it is not required.

**A Few Principles I Want to Reinforce**

- **No retaliation — ever. **If you report harassment in good faith, or cooperate in an investigation, you are protected. Any employee who retaliates against someone who made a report or supported one will face disciplinary action up to and including termination. This applies regardless of who the person is or what role they hold.

- **This applies everywhere we work. **The policy covers our office, client sites, conferences, business travel, company events, and any work-related communication — including Slack, email, text messages, and social media. If you are engaging with a colleague in any work context, this policy applies.

- **If you see something, say something. **Supervisors and managers have an affirmative duty to report suspected harassment — but every employee is encouraged to. You do not need to be the target of the behavior to report it.

- **Confidentiality is a priority. **All complaints will be investigated promptly, thoroughly, and as confidentially as possible. Due process protections apply to everyone involved.

**Questions**

If anything in the policy is unclear, or if you want to talk through how it applies to a specific situation, my door is open. Reach me at justin@propellant.media or 1 (877) 776-7358.

Thank you for your attention to this. Building the right culture is something we do together.
