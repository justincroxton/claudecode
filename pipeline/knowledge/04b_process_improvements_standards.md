| **1. Pre-Submission Contamination Checklist** |
| --- |

This is the single most critical quality gate. Cross-contamination from prior proposals has been our #1 disqualifying error — Colorado School of Mines content in a Carlow proposal, EKU references in a UNCP proposal, and 'Dear Vergel' salutations addressed to the wrong institution. Run every check below before converting to PDF.

- **Grep for ALL prior institution names: **Colorado School of Mines, EKU, Parkland, Carlow, Collin, CU Boulder, Montana, and any other institution from the last 10 submissions

- **Search for prior contact names: **e.g., 'Dear Vergel' or any salutation that doesn't match the current RFP's procurement contact

- **Search for ****'****NEEDS YOU****'**** flags: **These are internal placeholders that must never appear in a submitted document

- **Search for junk text: **'sdasd', 'asdf', 'xxx', 'TODO', or any placeholder characters from drafting

- **Search for duplicate salutations: **A prior greeting sitting above the correct one in the cover letter

- **Search for stray characters: **'222', random numbers, or orphaned text near images and page breaks

- **Verify every client in the portfolio table: **Only include clients we can back up with a reference contact or case study. No unsubstantiated names.

- **Verify phone number: **Must be 1 (877) 776-7358 on title page, cover letter, and all contact blocks. Never the personal (404) number.

- **Verify founding year: **2015 everywhere — including Aaron King's resume (was incorrectly listed as October 2014).

- **Verify CRM name consistency: **The institution's CRM must be named correctly throughout the entire document — personnel table, call staffing table, methodology, and case studies.

| **2. Formatting ****&**** Design Standards** |
| --- |

- **Font: **Poppins 10pt body (20 half-points). H1: 13pt. H2: 11pt. H3: 10.5pt. Brand red #e63412 for all headers and table headers. Black body text.

- **Subsection dividers: **Single-cell table with red bottom border (size 8, #e63412), no other borders. Title text is Poppins 13pt bold red. Margins: top 40, bottom 80, left 0, right 0. Copy-paste safe across Word and Google Docs.

- **Section dividers: **Full blank page with section title centered horizontally and vertically. This is critical — we copy and paste sections into our custom format with graphical dividers. The blank page serves as the insertion point.

- **Bullet points: **Must be Google Doc friendly. Use standard bullet formatting that survives copy-paste. Match the format of our past submitted RFPs.

- **Table of Contents: **Always include with dot leaders to page numbers. Update all page numbers before PDF conversion.

- **Table rows: **Alternating shading — odd rows WHITE (#FFFFFF), even rows super light grey (#F5F5F5). Header row always brand red with white bold text.

- **Paragraph spacing: **After: 120 (not 60). Apply to all body paragraphs.

- **Graphic placeholders: **Add red italic placeholder text for sections where a visual should be inserted: CRM Dashboard Samples, Budget Reconciliation Example, Media Plan Graphic, Monday.com Screenshot, Creative Ad Samples.

| **3. CRM Reference Rules** |
| --- |

- **Confirm the institution****'****s CRM before drafting: **Use the correct name (Slate, TargetX, Salesforce, Workday, REACH) consistently throughout the entire document

- **Check every location CRM is mentioned: **Personnel table, call staffing table, methodology section, CRM integration section, case studies, and analytics section

- **Case study CRM references: **When referencing a different institution's CRM (e.g., 'Slate CRM data' in a KSU case study), add a parenthetical: '(KSU's CRM)' so the committee doesn't confuse it with the current institution's system

- **Aaron King resume: **Must say 'October 2015 – Present' and reference the correct CRM for the current proposal's institution

| **4. Pricing Section Standards** |
| --- |

- **Alternative pricing models: **When the RFP asks for a pricing model we don't use (e.g., tuition revenue share), reference the Q&A addendum authorization to propose an alternative. Never leave their pricing form completely blank — include it with a cross-reference to our supplemental pricing table.

- **Standard model: **15% of media spend managed. Present in a clear table showing what's included (reporting, analytics, CRM integration, weekly calls) vs. what's additional (creative services, optional modules).

- **Creative services: **Scope and estimate with a monthly range based on program count and production needs. Never leave open-ended.

- **Investment summary: **Always include an Estimated Annual Investment Summary table with at least 3 years (Year 1, Year 2, Year 3) showing media spend, management fee, setup (waived), creative, and total.

- **Unspent media policy: **State explicitly that unspent media dollars are credited back. Bold this statement.

| **5. Student Support ****&**** Helpdesk Framing** |
| --- |

When an RFP requires responses to services outside our core (helpdesk, student support, faculty support), never lead with a 'fee-for-service disclaimer.' Lead with what we actually deliver.

- **Lead with our model: **24-hour inquiry response, weekly standing calls, real-time Looker Studio dashboards, melt-prevention retargeting campaigns, informed prospects who arrive at the admissions team already understanding program requirements

- **Frame 24/7 helpdesk as digital infrastructure: **Landing pages available 24/7, dashboards available 24/7, chatbot (if activated) available 24/7, plus human responsiveness during business hours. Not a tiered call center we don't have.

- **Position add-ons at the bottom: **AI chatbot, extended inquiry management, faculty content coordination — these are optional modules, not the headline. The core support model is included in the base engagement.

- **Use the framing: **'[Institution] does not need a vendor call center standing between prospective students and the university.' Adapt to each institution's name.

- **Never skip required sections: **If the Q&A says all sections must be addressed, address them. Frame non-core services as fee-for-service add-ons rather than skipping and risking disqualification.

| **6. Case Study Standards** |
| --- |

- **Hard numbers required: **Every case study must include a results table with CPL, lead volume, conversion rates, and time periods. No case study goes out without quantified outcomes.

- **KEY TAKEAWAY callout box: **Every case study ends with a colored callout box (amber background #FFF8E1, bold label 'KEY TAKEAWAY') tying the result back to the specific institution we're proposing to.

- **Optimization in Action sidebar: **Include at least one sidebar showing a mid-campaign data-driven decision (e.g., KSU Fintech LinkedIn reallocation: 20% budget shift, 34% application start increase). This demonstrates strategic thinking, not just execution.

- **Match institution type first: **Lead with the case study that mirrors the institution's type and size. KSU and Utah State are supplemental — don't lead with them when the RFP signals preference for comparable-size institutions.

| **7. Required Sections in Every Proposal** |
| --- |

Regardless of whether the RFP explicitly requests them, the following sections must appear in every Propellant Media proposal. If the RFP prescribes a required format, bake these into the existing structure as subsections.

- **Media Plan: **Channel allocation table with dollar amounts, 3 opening paragraphs (institution challenge, media philosophy, channel mix rationale), and Application Projections sub-section

- **Analytics ****&**** Reporting: **'What We Track' table and 'Reporting Cadence' table with deliverables, frequency, and descriptions

- **Competitive Set Analysis: **Named competitors in the institution's market with a competitive positioning table. Even if preliminary, include with a note that full report comes during Phase 1 Discovery

- **SWOT Analysis: **Color-coded 2x2 table in the Competitive Set section. Strengths = green (#E8F5E9/#2E7D32). Weaknesses = red (#FCE8E4/#e63412). Opportunities = blue (#E3F2FD/#1565C0). Threats = amber (#FFF8E1/#E65100). 2-3 bullets per quadrant. Never reuse across proposals.

- **CRM Integration ****&**** Attribution: **Three-step framework (Pixel-to-Ping, Audience Mirroring, Application Yield Reporting). Name the institution's specific CRM. ~2 pages. Required on every proposal.

- **Creative Process: **Include even if not explicitly requested. Show our creative capability with sample links and production process.

| **8. Table of Contents ****&**** Document Structure** |
| --- |

- **Default: **Use our standard TOC and section order (Cover Letter, Executive Summary, Scope of Services, Company Overview, Account Team, then methodology sections).

- **RFP-prescribed format: **If the Questions Answered Document analysis shows the RFP prescribes a REQUIRED response format, use their format instead. Map our standard sections into their structure.

- **Non-negotiable additions: **When using the RFP's required format, always find a way to include CRM Integration & Attribution, Analytics & Reporting, Competitive Set Analysis, Media Plan, and Creative Process — either as standalone subsections or woven into required sections where they naturally fit.

- **Never drop sections: **Just because the RFP didn't name them explicitly doesn't mean they should be excluded. These sections are where we differentiate.

- **TOC format: **Dot leaders between section titles and page numbers. Bold section titles in brand red. Appendix sub-entries listed. Update all page numbers before PDF conversion.

| **9. Scoring ****&**** Self-Evaluation** |
| --- |

- **Score against the rubric: **If the RFP provides a scoring rubric (point-based or weighted criteria), score our draft proposal against that rubric before submission. We will be evaluated on their criteria — grade ourselves the same way.

- **Narrative consensus evaluation: **When no numerical rubric exists, flag this in the Questions Answered Document and note that the proposal needs deeper institutional customization since there's no point system to optimize around.

- **Target scores: **Aim for 85+ on our internal scoring. Submissions below 80 should be reviewed for contamination, missing sections, or weak customization before going out.

| **10. Repetition ****&**** Tone Rules** |
| --- |

- **'****Why Propellant Media****'**** bullets: **These credential bullets belong in the Cover Letter or Executive Summary ONLY. Never restate the same six bullets verbatim in methodology sections, case studies, or pricing.

- **Natural weaving: **Differentiators should be woven naturally into the relevant sections. Mention Google Premier Partner when discussing Google Ads strategy. Mention MBE certification when discussing the institution's diversity mission. If it feels like boasting, it probably is.

- **Strategic repetition is fine: **Reinforcing a key theme across multiple sections (e.g., 'conversion from admitted to enrolled') is good. Restating the same bullet block is not.

- **Tone: **Professional but conversational. Confident, not arrogant. The institution is always the hero. Use 'partner' instead of 'vendor.' Mirror the RFP's own language.

| **11. Scope ****&**** Bid Decision Rules** |
| --- |

- **Never skip required sections: **Even if outside our core. Frame non-core services as fee-for-service add-ons.

- **Read the Q****&****A addendum: **Check for signals about what the institution actually wants vs. what the RFP template asks for. UNCP said 'enrollment marketing specialist, not OPM' — that changes the entire positioning.

- **OPM-adjacent RFPs: **When scope extends beyond media buying (helpdesk, student support, faculty support, market research), position as strategic marketing partner alternative to an OPM. Don't pretend to be something we're not.

- **Bid/no-bid scoring: **Use the 5-criteria scoring system (Sector fit, Case study strength, Budget potential, Competitive advantage, Compliance complexity) with the 22-25 = pursue aggressively, 18-21 = pursue if bandwidth allows, below 18 = do not pursue thresholds.

| **12. Video Proposal Standards** |
| --- |

- **Record for every submission: **Loom video proposal overview. Colin Owens flagged this as a unique differentiator. No other agency in our competitive set does this.

- **Length: **Under 2 minutes 15 seconds. Respect their time.

- **Structure: **Hook with their problem (0:00-0:20), reflect the RFP back (0:20-0:50), three differentiators (0:50-1:20), hard numbers from case studies (1:20-1:50), confident close (1:50-2:00).

- **Screen share: **Show the proposal cover page and key tables briefly. Don't just talk to camera the whole time.

- **CRM walkthrough: **Include a separate Loom video in the analytics section showing a live CRM dashboard example with attribution data. Link it inline.

- **Placement: **Proposal overview link goes in the Cover Letter. CRM walkthrough link goes in the Analytics & Reporting section.

| **13. Document Assembly ****&**** Submission** |
| --- |

- **Signed RFP body: **When RFPs require the entire signed RFP body returned with the submission, those pages are pass-through compliance — not part of our proposal content page count.

- **Pricing forms: **Always include the institution's required pricing form (e.g., Attachment A) even if proposing an alternative model. Never leave their form completely blank — include a cross-reference.

- **File naming: **Check the portal for file naming conventions before uploading. Some portals reject files with special characters.

- **Upload early: **Upload to the portal at least 2 hours before deadline. eVP and similar portals can have upload issues under load.

- **Confirmation: **Obtain confirmation of receipt from the procurement contact or portal system after submission.
