# Propellant Media — RFP Qualification Template

The standard question set used to qualify/score an RFP and to outline a response.
Most questions are **common to every vertical**; each vertical adds a few of its
own. `/daily-rfps` embeds the relevant vertical block under each opportunity's
`TEMPLATE` divider, and the "Bid Decisions" Claude Project answers them against
the full RFP document.

When the vertical isn't Higher Education or Government, use **COMMON + the closest
vertical add-on** (Tourism, Airports, Public Health, Healthcare below).

## COMMON QUESTIONS (every vertical)

**Identification**
1. Official name of the file/solicitation reviewed?
2. Name of the issuing entity (and, if relevant, department/division)?
3. Submission due date?
4. Submission **and** question/answer (Q&A) deadlines?
5. Submission method — online portal, email, or hard-copy mail?

**Mandatory actions & compliance**
6. What compliance documents/requirements MUST we factor?
7. Is there a pre-proposal meeting? If so, is it MANDATORY?
8. Any other mandatory action items to be aware of?
9. Do we need to notarize anything?
10. What documents/addenda must be signed and submitted with the proposal
    (exhibits, addenda, forms)?
11. Anything that could PROHIBIT us from responding (in-state business
    registration, many addenda, in-state location preference)?
12. Do we need to register/apply as a vendor in the state? How much lead time?
13. Is there an in-state location requirement or preference? Do we lose points?
14. Final compliance checklist to ensure the submission is complete.

**Scope & intent**
15. True scope of services, listed line by line.
16. Main services requested — what are they truly pronouncing in the document?
17. Which single service do they seem to want above all others?
18. Core objective of the client.
19. Which digital channels did they highlight?
20. Campaign timeline (start and end dates).
21. Budget / estimated contract value?
22. Is there an incumbent agency?

**Scoring & response strategy**
23. Scoring rubric details — and which rubric questions MUST be addressed?
24. Is there a max page limit? Where in the document is it discussed? What counts
    toward it and what's excluded?
25. Given the page limit and our past submissions, which key sections should we keep?
26. If there were 5 key sections we must absolutely include, what should they be?
27. Are we required to follow a specific response outline, or is there flexibility?
28. What specific language did they repeat/emphasize that we should mirror in our
    technical response?
29. Is DEI status or a certification a benefit/scored advantage?
30. What can Propellant bring that differentiates us from the competition?
31. 5–6 differentiation points to pepper throughout the response — what makes us
    stand apart / what to highlight?
32. **Bottom line: would Propellant Media be a good fit for this response? (BID / MAYBE / PASS + why)**

## HIGHER EDUCATION — add-ons
- H1. Name of the university/college.
- H2. Is this for graduate or undergraduate studies (or a specific program/school)?
- H3. Aside from higher-ed experience, which 5–6 points should we pepper to
  differentiate (enrollment outcomes, recruitment funnels, geofencing campuses,
  yield, etc.)?

**Higher-ed creative / messaging POV (esp. graduate / MBA):**
1. Lead with career outcomes — promotion, leadership, advancement (not just "earn your MBA").
2. Keep it short and mobile-friendly — one strong hook, one benefit, one clear CTA.
3. Speak to working professionals — flexibility, balance, real-world impact.
4. Use a clear, low-friction CTA — "Learn More" / "Explore the Program" beats pushing "Apply Now" too early.

## GOVERNMENT — add-ons
- G1. Name of the government entity.
- G2. What state is this in — any state-specific compliance concerns?
- G3. Is this print, digital, billboard, out-of-home, or other marketing services?
- G4. Does PR (public relations) appear to be a core request? What about community engagement?
- G5. Is there a request for creative ad development?
- G6. What type of promotion is this (e.g., public health, disease awareness, tourism, recruitment)?
- G7. What types of case studies would help us win this RFP?

## TOURISM — add-ons (DMO / CVB / tourism office)
- T1. Name of the DMO/CVB/tourism office.
- T2. Is this destination brand marketing, media buying/placement, or full AOR?
- T3. Target visitor segments (leisure vs. business, drive vs. fly markets, key feeder markets)?
- T4. Seasonal/always-on? Any co-op or partner marketing component?
- T5. Channels emphasized (OTT/CTV, paid social, search, OOH, content/PR)?
- T6. Is funding tied to lodging/occupancy tax (affects budget cadence/reporting)?
- T7. Case studies that win: destination/tourism campaigns with visitation or
  attribution lift.

## AIRPORTS — add-ons
- A1. Name of the airport / authority.
- A2. Is this air-service-development marketing, passenger/destination advertising,
  or general brand? (Exclude pure ad-concession/space-lease RFPs.)
- A3. Goal — route launch/retention, enplanements, catchment-region awareness?
- A4. Key stakeholders (airlines, region, FAA considerations)?
- A5. Case studies that win: aviation/airport or destination-region campaigns.

## PUBLIC HEALTH — add-ons
- P1. Issuing health agency/department/coalition.
- P2. Campaign topic/behavior change (tobacco/vaping, vaccine, mental health,
  traffic safety, etc.)?
- P3. Target population & health-equity / multicultural / in-language requirements?
- P4. Media buying + creative + evaluation/metrics expectations (reach, CDC-style KPIs)?
- P5. Is it grant-funded (affects budget/reporting/compliance)?
- P6. Case studies that win: behavior-change / public-health media campaigns.
- (DEI/multicultural capability is frequently scored here — emphasize it.)

## HEALTHCARE — add-ons
- C1. Issuing org type (hospital, health system, payer, clinic, senior living, behavioral health).
- C2. Is this service-line marketing, brand, or patient acquisition?
- C3. Target — patient volume, specific service line, competitive market dynamics?
- C4. HIPAA / regulated-content considerations for creative and data?
- C5. Case studies that win: healthcare patient-acquisition / service-line growth.
