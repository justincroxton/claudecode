Frequently Asked Questions

What is geofencing marketing?

Geofencing draws a virtual boundary around a real-world location. When a person's device enters that boundary, they can be added to a targetable audience and served your ads across the apps and websites they use — long after they've left the location.

How is addressable geofencing different?

Addressable geofencing targets specific household and business addresses using plat-line data from property tax and public land records. This lets us target up to 1 million precise addresses per campaign, rather than a general radius.

Can I track whether it actually drives visits?

Yes. We place "conversion zones" around your physical locations to measure the uplift in foot traffic from people who saw your ads — connecting your ad spend directly to real-world visits.

What kind of ads can I run?

Static display banners, native ads, video, and OTT/Connected TV (such as Roku and Hulu) — all served to the same geofenced audience for a true omnichannel campaign.

Is it privacy-compliant?

Yes. Campaigns use anonymized, aggregated device data and adhere to industry privacy standards and applicable regulations. No personally identifiable information is shared with advertisers.

What's the minimum budget and contract?

We tailor recommendations to your goals on a discovery call. Most programs are flexible with no long-term contract required — ask us for a custom proposal.
